# Stabilize native indicator buttons and restore tooltip feedback

## Goal and evidence

The owner reports that installed Plan 150 generally works, but buttons change
position and descriptions appear absent. Compare the unchanged MIT Omarchy
parents at ac2690cd7be4f47e4a255f5e2e78d904d436b068 with the native provider,
original active/inactive projections, tooltip admission and the staged English
catalog. Do not infer native hover success from prior direct-signal/RPC fixtures.

## Gaps and proposed shape

The provider currently clears all known states after every action, destroying
both projections before readback and resetting active ordering. Original grouping
also inherently moves a control when its active/inactive membership changes.
Eight-second reads disable the whole Item through writable rather than only
presses, disturbing hover. Four original translation keys are missing locally.
Investigate actual hover/tooltip admission in a contained scene before deciding
whether any additional deferred-hover seam is needed.

Keep original vendor files, IndicatorBlock/Loader/BarIndicator/WidgetButton markup,
shared tooltip host, original gestures and default grouping policy intact. Add a
narrow opt-in stable-controls mode to the original indicator derivation for the
two native controls: reuse the existing ordered block and single-item semantics,
keep known buttons in fixed slots, and dim inactive controls rather than moving
between groups. Unknown owners still have no admitted false/inactive placeholder.
This explicitly departs from active-first grouping for these two controls only;
it is not a new/replacement panel or an alteration of generic original defaults.

Keep observed components stable through successful actions/readback, never turn
an empty observation into inactive state. Maintain no-queue/proof-bound native
requests and uncertainty handling. Separate pressability from hover availability
while busy/blocked. Admit only the four pinned original English labels; preserve
translation catalog provenance. If reproduced, narrowly synchronize tooltips once
hover/opacity/text are observable instead of weakening original tooltip guards.

## Outcome and retained evidence

Source-only correction complete. Original licensed parents and default generic
behavior are unchanged. The two known native controls explicitly use original
ordered/single-block slots, always visible with original inactive dimming instead
of active-first regrouping. Genuine provider loss/readmission may change membership
and rebuild the original model; there is no fake inactive placeholder or promise
of stable targets across unknown state. No alternative authored panel was added.

Confirmed actions keep last observed delegates/value through readback with a
separate no-write fence; uncertainty/malformed/old-scope results revoke admission.
Busy/blocked controls remain hoverable, not actionable. Four exact English labels
come from the pinned MIT catalog, SHA-256
c34e291f8309073442ed9d2cfa971877b04c1bd4eaea9462f2f3906fce758584;
its full catalog stays private and is not staged. The real original MouseArea and
tooltip host admitted first hover in the contained baseline; a pre-fix native
UI witness then reproduced a stale hovered action description. Only observed-text
refresh was added; original tooltip guards/delay/handoff were not weakened.

Private evidence: work/indicator-feedback-151/ contains verified catalog origin,
source delta, unit/regression logs and verification.json. Nineteen backup records
retain hashes/modes/absence. All 142 source seals verify, with only the three
reviewed authored and three derived seals changed. Transform revisions are
original-indicators-ui-v2, native-dnd-indicator-v2 and native-nightlight-indicator-v2;
BarIndicator and all upstream parent bytes remain unchanged.

Final main checkpoint: 1,535 units; 39 contained frame cases; audio 32, tooltip 8,
